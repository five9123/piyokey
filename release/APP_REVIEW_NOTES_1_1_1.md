Version 1.1.1 is a Game Center reliability hotfix.

No app account or demo credentials are required. Game Center is optional and uses the reviewer's own Apple Game Center account. Core learning and games work without Game Center sign-in.

After the three guided onboarding missions, open the Game tab. Flow, Word Rain, Initials Quiz, Word Quiz and Dictation each provide Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced official ranked presets. In Settings, choose the built-in Dubeolsik keyboard, Korean 10-key keyboard, or Korean OS keyboard. All these inputs are eligible for the same game/difficulty leaderboard. Hardware keyboards use the OS input path.

At the end of a ranked game, the score card shows the ranking button and registration status after the result animation. The button is present for lower scores and zero scores as well as new records. A successful submission is followed by a leaderboard entry read-back; the app confirms registration when the returned player score is at least the retained eligible best. If confirmation is delayed or unavailable, the app retains the result and offers bounded automatic and manual retry. Game Center shows the best score for each board rather than an entry for every session.

Weekly Piyo Cup, available directly from Home or the Game tab, uses only its weekly leaderboard. Regular Flow uses only its difficulty-specific classic leaderboard. Their local best scores and new-record comparisons are separate. Weekly submission is tied to the loaded occurrence.

The existing optional lifetime typee pro purchase unlocks unlimited local user decks plus on-device deck creation and editing. Up to three user decks and core practice/games remain free. There is no account registration, subscription, advertising, external checkout or public deck upload.
